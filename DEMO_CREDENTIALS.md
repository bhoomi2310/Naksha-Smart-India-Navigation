# Demo Credentials

For testing and demonstration purposes, you can use the following demo account:

**Email:** `demo@naksha.app`

## How to Use

1. **From Landing Page:**
   - Click "Sign In" or "Sign In with Demo" button
   - The demo credentials will be displayed in the login modal
   - Click "Use Demo Account" button to auto-fill the credentials
   - Click "Sign In" to login

2. **From Login Page:**
   - Click "Use Demo Account" button in the login modal
   - Credentials will be auto-filled
   - Click "Sign In" to login

3. **Manual Entry:**
   - Enter `demo@naksha.app` as email
   - Enter `demo123` as password
   - Click "Sign In"

## Notes

- The demo account is automatically created when the backend server starts
- The demo account has full access to all features
- You can also create your own account using the registration form
